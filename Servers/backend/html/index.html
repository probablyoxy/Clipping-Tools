What is Clipping Tools?

By linking with the Discord bot, Clipping Tools#8377, the app watches your active voice channels to see who you are talking with. When you hit your clip button and send the signal, it instantly distributes it to everyone connected in your VC, triggering their software to record at the exact same time. Now you can capture everyone's perspective of the same moment, no matter what!
